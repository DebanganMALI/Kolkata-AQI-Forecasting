How it works. Training data: official CPCB city AQI (2018 to 2026), 7 WBPCB monitoring stations, Open-Meteo weather and NASA FIRMS fires. Eight forecasters were compared; the GRU was chosen on the 2024 validation year and tested once on 2025 to 2026. The live forecast uses a separate GRU trained only on inputs the live feed provides (city AQI and weather); its own test score is shown above.
